Quick guide

Feeding basics

Newborn feeding needs vary. Follow your baby's cues and the advice of your healthcare professional. If breastfeeding is difficult or painful, seek qualified feeding support.

Remember: Do not give a newborn water, honey or other foods/fluids unless advised by a qualified healthcare professional.
Quick guide

Safe sleep basics

  • Place baby on the back for sleep.
  • Use a firm, flat sleep surface with a fitted sheet.
  • Keep pillows, loose blankets and soft objects out of the sleep area.
  • Keep baby's face and head uncovered.
  • Avoid sleeping with baby on a sofa or armchair.
  • Follow individualized medical advice when applicable.
Quick guide

Hygiene, cord & skin care

Wash your hands before handling the baby. Keep the umbilical cord clean and dry according to your healthcare team's instructions. Avoid applying oils, powders, herbs or other substances to the cord unless specifically advised.

Important

When to seek medical help

Seek urgent medical evaluation if a newborn has trouble breathing, blue/grey colour, is difficult to wake, has a seizure, is feeding poorly, repeatedly vomits, or has a fever or other concerning change.

For a newborn with possible serious illness, don't rely on an online checklist—contact an appropriate healthcare professional or emergency service promptly.
